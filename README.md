## 📚 Peer-Auditable Preprint Series (Registered DOIs via CERN / Zenodo)

Every publication in the **VTI Research** archive strictly adheres to international scholarly publishing standards (**COPE / ICMJE / ACM / IEEE / arXiv 2026**):
- **Strict Human Authorship Warrant:** Formal authorship (`Author` / `\author{}`) is held exclusively by the human investigator (**Caio Gonçalves**), maintaining full claim-level accountability.
- **Transparent Symbiotic Engineering Disclosure:** Cross-family AI adversarial review and deterministic oracle verification are documented in each manuscript's **Adapted CRediT Disclosure** section.
- **Separation of Formats & Bilingual Access:** Each research work is split into a standalone **Executive Brief** (for C-Level, CISOs, CFOs, and Governance Boards) and a standalone **Two-Column Scientific Paper** (IMRaD / Systems Engineering format), available in both **English (`EN-US`)** and **Portuguese (`PT-BR`)**, alongside complete **LaTeX + BibTeX (`.zip`)** sources.
- **Strict Third-Party Anonymization:** All industrial architectural evaluations and production case studies enforce strict mathematical abstraction of third-party identities, network topologies, and credentials.
