\begin{abstract}
Large Language Model (LLM) agentic systems suffer systematic degradation over extended operational horizons due to three fundamental classes of cognitive failure: non-uniform long-context attention decay (\emph{Lost in the Middle}), statistical override of newly enacted rules by high-frequency historical priors (\emph{Override Failure}), and persistent memory pollution from transient runtime artifacts (\emph{Contaminated Mindware}). Motivated by a precursor forensic audit across $20$ real-world queries in a production enterprise tax, fiscal, and labor advisory platform ($\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{Hub}}$)---where generative assistants lacking epistemic governance exhibited a $20\%$ ($4/20$) critical failure rate---this paper formalizes the \textbf{Dual-Axis Bitemporal Neuro-Cognitive Architecture of \texttt{MemoryOS}} within the \textbf{VTI OS System} ecosystem. The architecture couples five functional memory tiers plus a cryptographically quarantined aversive store (\textbf{Axis~1}) with a tripartite executive governance framework inspired by Keith E. Stanovich (\textbf{Axis~2}: Autonomous Mind, Algorithmic Mind grounded in deterministic oracles, and Reflective Mind structured into two-phase blind physical auditing and cross-family collegiate deliberation). Grounded in \textbf{32 unique peer-reviewed scientific studies} and statutory data-protection mandates, the model introduces: (i) append-only cognitive records over Snodgrass half-open bitemporal intervals $[\texttt{valid\_from}, \texttt{valid\_to}) \times [\texttt{tx\_from}, \texttt{tx\_to})$ with linear-time Tarjan SCC acyclicity verification in $O(|V| + |E|)$; (ii) a tri-state epistemic quarantine sealed by domain-separated SHA-256 Merkle Trees; (iii) a multidimensional post-RRF epistemic reranker assigning an infinite half-life ($T_{1/2} = \infty$) to active canonical rules; (iv) an 8-dimensional conflict-monitoring matrix (\texttt{dACC}); and (v) an idempotent offline consolidation cycle ($f(f(S)) = f(S)$). Evaluated on a reproducible $N = 50$ bitemporal conflict \emph{Golden Benchmark}, the epistemic reranker elevated $\mathrm{NDCG@5}$ from $0.5764$ to $1.0000$ ($+73.49\%$) and $\mathrm{MRR}$ from $0.4000$ to $1.0000$ ($+150.00\%$), reducing revocation override failures from $40/50$ to $0/50$ ($\mathrm{CI}_{95\%}^{\mathrm{Wilson}} \approx [0.9289, 1.0000]$) at a monotonic $\mathrm{P95}$ latency of $0.34\,\mathrm{ms}$ ($0.44\,\mathrm{ms}$ under complex enterprise load).

\section{Introduction and Long-Horizon Cognitive Failure Modes}

The transition of Large Language Models (LLMs) from stateless chat interfaces into autonomous software engineering, regulatory analysis, and enterprise decision-making agents exposes a structural gap between \emph{local generative fluency} and \emph{long-horizon epistemic consistency}. While modern Transformer architectures synthesize coherent prose within isolated turns, multi-session operation across evolving schemas and statutory amendments suffers predictable reliability decay across three primary failure modes:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,nosep,itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Long-Context Attention Decay (\emph{Lost in the Middle}):} Expanding nominal context windows does not yield uniform recall. Liu et al.~\cite{E03} proved that LLM attention exhibits a U-shaped curve favoring primacy and recency boundaries while severely attenuating middle-positioned tokens. Concatenating raw session logs inflates latency and dilutes critical safety constraints~\cite{E01,E02,E04}.
    \item \textbf{Statistical Override of Enacted Rules (\emph{Override Failure}):} In non-stationary domains (tax law, labor statutes, evolving software architectures), truth is parameterized by validity intervals $[v_{\mathrm{from}}, v_{\mathrm{to}})$. When a widely cited legacy rule $d_{\mathrm{old}}$ is formally superseded by a newly enacted canonical rule $d_{\mathrm{new}}$, parametric priors $P_{\theta}(y \mid q)$ remain heavily skewed toward $d_{\mathrm{old}}$. Conventional RAG engines using lexical/vector similarity or uniform recency decay~\cite{E08} allow high-density superseded documents or recent logs mentioning legacy rules to override the active norm---instantiating Keith E. Stanovich's \emph{Override Failure}~\cite{E20,E21}.
    \item \textbf{Persistent Memory Pollution (\emph{Contaminated Mindware}):} Agents storing verbal self-reflections~\cite{E18} without epistemic quarantine routinely promote transient runtime glitches (momentary DNS failures, ephemeral port locks, truncated stack traces) into permanent system-prompt prohibitions, creating what Stanovich terms \emph{Contaminated Mindware}~\cite{E20,E22}.
\end{enumerate}

Crucially, intrinsic verbal prompting cannot resolve these pathologies: Huang et al.~\cite{E28} demonstrated that LLMs cannot reliably self-correct reasoning without external grounded signals, frequently amplifying confirmation bias (\emph{Myside Bias}). Reliable error correction requires deterministic tool-interactive critiquing~\cite{E29} beyond ungrounded iterative refinement~\cite{E30}, complemented by multi-agent adversarial debate~\cite{E31} and deliberate tree search~\cite{E32}.

\section{Formal Dual-Axis Architecture and the 10 Cognitive Pillars}

Let an agentic system $\mathcal{A}$ operate across discrete sessions $s_1, \dots, s_m$. \texttt{MemoryOS} decomposes $\mathcal{A} = \langle \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{Axis\,1}}, \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{Axis\,2}} \rangle$ into \textbf{Axis~1} (5+1 memory tiers: Working, Episodic, Bitemporal Semantic, Local Project Scope \texttt{.agents/PROJECT\_MEMORY.md}, Procedural \texttt{SKILLS.md}, and Quarantined Aversive \texttt{DEAD\_ENDS.md} + Merkle Store) and \textbf{Axis~2} (Stanovich's Autonomous, Algorithmic, and Reflective governance rings), summarized in Table~\ref{tab:pillars}.

\subsection{Pillar 1: Working Memory and Primacy-Recency Paging}
Grounded in Baddeley's bounded working memory~\cite{E01,E02}, Liu et al.'s U-shaped long-context attention profile~\cite{E03}, and MemGPT's virtual context paging~\cite{E04}, \texttt{backend/maestro/context\_assembler.js} solves a priority-ordered allocation over candidate blocks $\mathcal{B} = \{b_1, \dots, b_m\}$ subject to $\sum_{b_i \in \mathcal{S}} \ell(b_i) \le B_{\max}$. Safety invariants and validated \texttt{DEAD\_ENDS.md} constraints occupy the primacy prefix; active phase goals and canonical records occupy the recency suffix; and external artifacts are isolated inside \texttt{<retrieved\_evidence readonly="true">} tags with nested-delimiter escaping to prevent indirect prompt injection.

\subsection{Pillar 2: 4-Way Hybrid RRF and Post-RRF Epistemic Reranker}
Modeling hippocampal multi-cue recall~\cite{E05}, \texttt{backend/utils/rrf\_search\_engine.js} fuses four channels $V = \{\text{fts5}, \text{vector}, \text{entity}, \text{graph}\}$ using BM25~\cite{E06} ($w_{\text{fts5}}=1.0$), dense cosine similarity ($w_{\text{vector}}=1.0$), IDF entity scores ($w_{\text{entity}}=0.8$), and associative triples ($w_{\text{graph}}=0.6$) via weighted Reciprocal Rank Fusion~\cite{E07} ($k = 60$). Over executed channels $V_{\mathrm{exec}} \subseteq V$, the raw RRF score and exact theoretical ceiling are:
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{rrf}}^{\mathrm{raw}}(q,d) = \sum_{v \in V_{\mathrm{exec}}} \frac{w_v}{k + \mathrm{rank}_v(d)}, \quad
C_{\mathrm{RRF}}(V_{\mathrm{exec}}) = \frac{\sum_{v \in V_{\mathrm{exec}}} w_v}{k + 1}
\end{equation}
yielding normalized adherence $S_{\mathrm{rrf}}(q,d) = \mathrm{clamp}(S_{\mathrm{rrf}}^{\mathrm{raw}}(q,d) / C_{\mathrm{RRF}}(V_{\mathrm{exec}}), 0, 1)$.

To overcome uniform temporal decay~\cite{E08} (under which a 2-year-old enacted statute decays to $(1 + 0.05 \times 730)^{-1} \approx 0.0267$ and loses to a 30-minute debug log), \texttt{MemoryOS} introduces a gated post-RRF epistemic reranking family. While its general conceptual formulation separates admissibility gates from internal relevance, importance $I(d)$, and decay $D(d, t_{\mathrm{ref}})$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Score}_{\mathrm{conc}}(q,d,t_{\mathrm{ref}}) &= \mathbb{I}(S_{\mathrm{sem}} \ge \theta_{\min}) \cdot F_{\mathrm{valid}}(d, t_{\mathrm{ref}}) \\
&\quad \cdot A(d, \mathrm{intent}) \cdot \bigl[\alpha S_{\mathrm{rrf}} + \beta I(d) + \gamma D(d, t_{\mathrm{ref}})\bigr],
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
the production kernel (\texttt{backend/utils/epistemic\_reranker.js}) enforces exact multiplicative nullification of decayed logs coupled with governed Residual \emph{Boost-Only} Fusion:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
S_{\mathrm{final}}(q,d,t_{\mathrm{ref}}) &= \mathbb{I}\!\bigl(S_{\mathrm{rrf}}(q,d) \ge \theta_{\min}(\mathrm{intent})\bigr) \cdot S_{\mathrm{rank}}(q,d) \\
&\quad \cdot F_{\mathrm{valid}}(d, t_{\mathrm{ref}}, \mathrm{intent}) \cdot A(d, \mathrm{intent}) \cdot C(d) \cdot D(d, t_{\mathrm{ref}}).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:sfinal}
\end{equation}

Here: (1)~$\theta_{\min}(\text{normative}) = 0.15$ and $\theta_{\min}(\text{other}) = 0.08$ reject off-topic high-authority documents; (2)~given secondary ordinal score $S_B \in [0,1]$, gain $\gamma_B = 0.35$, and boundary switch $g_B \in \{0,1\}$ (locked to $g_B \equiv 0$ on corporate business surfaces), residual fusion computes:
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{rank}}(q,d) = S_{\mathrm{rrf}}(q,d) + \gamma_B \, g_B \, \bigl(1 - S_{\mathrm{rrf}}(q,d)\bigr) S_B(q,d).
\end{equation}
\begin{proposition}[Monotonic Non-Degradation]
For all $S_{\mathrm{rrf}}, S_B, \gamma_B \in [0,1]$ and $g_B \in \{0,1\}$, $S_{\mathrm{rrf}}(q,d) \le S_{\mathrm{rank}}(q,d) \le 1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Non-negativity of $\gamma_B g_B (1 - S_{\mathrm{rrf}}) S_B$ implies $S_{\mathrm{rank}} \ge S_{\mathrm{rrf}}$, while $\gamma_B g_B S_B \le 1$ yields $S_{\mathrm{rank}} \le S_{\mathrm{rrf}} + (1 - S_{\mathrm{rrf}}) = 1$.
\end{proof}
(3)~$F_{\mathrm{valid}}(d, t_{\mathrm{ref}}, \mathrm{intent}) = 1.0$ iff $\mathrm{status}(d)=\text{validated}$ and $v_{\mathrm{from}}(d) \le t_{\mathrm{ref}} < v_{\mathrm{to}}^{*}(d)$ ($0.85$ for explicit historical lineage; $0.0$ otherwise in strict normative mode); (4)~$A(d, \text{normative}) = 1.0 + 0.5(\mathrm{clamp}(a(d), 0.8, 1.5)-0.8)/0.7 \in [1.0, 1.5]$, and $A \equiv 1.0$ for non-normative intents; $C(d) \in [0,1]$; and (5)~tier-conditioned exponential decay over elapsed days $\Delta t$ is:
\begin{equation}
D(d, t_{\mathrm{ref}}) = \exp\!\left(-\frac{\ln 2}{T_{1/2}(\tau(d))} \Delta t\right),
\end{equation}
with $T_{1/2}(\text{canonical}) = +\infty \implies D \equiv 1.0$, $T_{1/2}(\text{project\_fact})=180\text{d}$, $T_{1/2}(\text{skill})=90\text{d}$, $T_{1/2}(\text{episodic/insight})=30\text{d}$, and $T_{1/2}(\text{log\_evidence})=3\text{d}$. Ties resolve deterministically by $(S_{\mathrm{final}} \downarrow, S_{\mathrm{rrf}} \downarrow, \mathrm{id}_{\mathrm{lex}} \uparrow)$.

\subsection{Pillar 3: Complementary Learning Systems and Idempotent Sleep}
Following McClelland et al.~\cite{E09}, Diekelmann \& Born~\cite{E10}, and HippoRAG~\cite{E11}, \texttt{backend/utils/dream\_scheduler.js} executes offline consolidation guarded by OS wake-drift cooldowns ($>45\,\mathrm{s}$) and a zero-token SQLite \emph{Delta Check}. Let $\Sigma(S) = (R_{\mathrm{cog}}, L_{\mathrm{exp}}, F_{\mathrm{sem}})$ denote canonical logical database state. At fixed reference time $t_0$, operator $f_{t_0}$ (\texttt{runIdempotentEpistemicDreamCycle}) transitions contaminated lessons to \texttt{rejected}, transient quarantines $>48\,\mathrm{h}$ to \texttt{expired}, and elapsed validated records ($v_{\mathrm{to}} \le t_0$) to \texttt{needs\_revalidation}, never auto-promoting quarantined items. Because entry and target states are disjoint, $f_{t_0}$ is an exact algebraic projection:
\begin{equation}
\forall S, \forall t_0: \quad f_{t_0}\bigl(f_{t_0}(S)\bigr) = f_{t_0}(S),
\end{equation}
verified via SHA-256 state digest equality $\mathcal{H}(\Sigma(f_{t_0}(f_{t_0}(S)))) = \mathcal{H}(\Sigma(f_{t_0}(S)))$.

\subsection{Pillar 4: Snodgrass Append-Only Bitemporality and Tarjan SCC}
Grounded in Snodgrass \& Ahn~\cite{E12} and temporal reasoning over dynamic graphs~\cite{E14}, \texttt{backend/utils/fact\_history\_manager.js} models \texttt{cognitive\_records} over orthogonal right-half-open intervals $[v_{\mathrm{from}}, v_{\mathrm{to}}) \times [t_{\mathrm{from}}, t_{\mathrm{to}})$. A point-in-time query at $(t_{\mathrm{ref}}, t_{\mathrm{tx}})$ returns:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{R}(t_{\mathrm{ref}}, t_{\mathrm{tx}}) &= \bigl\{ d \in \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{cog}} \;\big|\; \mathrm{status}(d)=\text{validated} \\
&\quad \land v_{\mathrm{from}}(d) \le t_{\mathrm{ref}} < v_{\mathrm{to}}^{*}(d) \land t_{\mathrm{from}}(d) \le t_{\mathrm{tx}} < t_{\mathrm{to}}^{*}(d) \bigr\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Under serialized SQLite \texttt{BEGIN IMMEDIATE} transactions, linking successor $d_{\mathrm{succ}}$ to predecessor $d_{\mathrm{pred}}$ atomically closes $v_{\mathrm{to}}(d_{\mathrm{pred}}) \leftarrow v_{\mathrm{from}}(d_{\mathrm{succ}})$ ($v_{\mathrm{from}}(d_{\mathrm{succ}}) > v_{\mathrm{from}}(d_{\mathrm{pred}})$) and executes Tarjan's depth-first SCC algorithm~\cite{E13} over $G=(V,E)$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{lowlink}[u] = \min\bigl(&\{\mathrm{index}[u]\} \cup \{\mathrm{lowlink}[w] \mid (u,w) \in E_{\mathrm{tree}}\} \\
&\cup \{\mathrm{index}[w] \mid (u,w) \in E_{\mathrm{back}} \land w \in \mathrm{Stack}\}\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Any SCC with $|C|>1$ or self-loop $(u,u)\in E$ raises \texttt{CyclicDependencyException} in $\Theta(|V|+|E|)$ time, guaranteeing a strict DAG.

\subsection{Pillar 5: Non-Declarative Skills, Quarantine, and Merkle Store}
Combining Squire's non-declarative taxonomy~\cite{E15}, LeDoux's regulated threat circuits~\cite{E16}, Voyager skill libraries~\cite{E17}, and Reflexion~\cite{E18} with Merkle's cryptographic trees~\cite{E19}, \texttt{MemoryOS} caps \texttt{SKILLS.md} at $2,500$ tokens and governs experiential failures via state space $Q = \{\text{quarantined}, \text{validated}, \text{rejected}, \text{expired}, \text{needs\_revalidation}\}$. Raw failures enter \texttt{quarantined}; promotion to \texttt{validated} (exported to \texttt{DEAD\_ENDS.md} $\le 1,000$ tokens) requires non-transience and recurrence $\ge 2$ across distinct sessions. Meanwhile, \texttt{backend/utils/evidence\_store.js} scrubs secrets and Modulo-11 PII~\cite{N01} and seals off-prompt JSONL records via domain-separated SHA-256 hashes:
\begin{align}
h_i^{\mathrm{leaf}} &= \mathrm{SHA256}\bigl(\texttt{"mos:merkle:leaf:v1:"} \,\|\, \mathrm{canon}(r_i)\bigr), \\
h_{u,v}^{\mathrm{node}} &= \mathrm{SHA256}\bigl(\texttt{"mos:merkle:node:v1:"} \,\|\, h_u \,\|\, \texttt{":"} \,\|\, h_v\bigr),
\end{align}
preventing second-preimage height confusion and supporting $\Theta(\log_2 N)$ inclusion proofs even after expired raw payloads are purged (\texttt{payload\_purged = 1}).

\subsection{Pillars 6--10: Stanovich Tripartite Governance, 8D \texttt{dACC} Matrix, Two-Phase Blind Audit, Cross-Family Council, and \texttt{Barrier P4.1}}
Extending dual-process theory~\cite{E23,E24}, Stanovich~\cite{E20,E21,E22} separates Type~1 Autonomous generation from Type~2 Algorithmic decoupling and Reflective epistemic regulation. Inspired by \texttt{dACC} conflict monitoring~\cite{E25}, fast conflict detection~\cite{E26}, and higher-order inductive biases~\cite{E27}, \texttt{backend/maestro/council\_risk\_matrix.js} computes in $<1\,\mathrm{ms}$:

R_{\mathrm{total}}(\mathbf{d}) = \sum_{j=1}^{8} w_j d_j, \quad d_j \in [0,4], \quad R_{\mathrm{total}} \in [0, 49.6],
\end{equation}
over Reversibility ($1.5$), Blast Radius ($1.2$), Data Sensitivity ($2.0$), Privileges/DDL ($1.8$), Memory Persistence ($1.6$), Financial/Legal Impact ($1.8$), Contract Breakage ($1.4$), and Missing Test Evidence ($1.2$), alongside absolute triggers $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{abs}} = \{\texttt{DDL\_MIGRATION}, \texttt{AUTH\_OR\_PRODUCTION\_CREDENTIALS}, \texttt{EVIDENCE\_STORE\_MUTATION}, \texttt{CORPORATE\_EPIC\_PRODUCTION\_RELEASE}\}$. Tasks route to \textbf{Level~1} ($R_{\mathrm{total}} < 8.0 \to$ Ring~1 symbolic AST/Mod-11/Git oracles~\cite{E29}), \textbf{Level~2} ($8.0 \le R_{\mathrm{total}} < 16.0 \to$ Rings~1+2), or \textbf{Level~3} ($R_{\mathrm{total}} \ge 16.0 \lor \mathcal{T}\cap\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{abs}}\neq\varnothing \to$ Rings~1+2+3).
